1. Kahan Par Track Karein? (Direct Websites & Portals)
Aapko in specific official portals ke Tenders / RFPs / EOI section par regular visit karna hoga:

A. GIL (Gujarat Informatics Limited)
Official Website: gil.gujarat.gov.in

Jahan Check Karna Hai: Homepage par "Tenders" ya "RFPs / EOIs" tab.

Kya Milta Hai: State-wide AI projects, e-Governance applications, Cloud Services, aur Empanelment notices.

B. Smart City Mission Portals (City-Wise)
Gujarat ki mukhya Smart City SPVs (Special Purpose Vehicles) alag se AI Chatbots, ML Traffic/Surveillance, aur Smart Dashboards ke EOI nikalte hain:

Ahmedabad (AHM Smart City / AMC): ahmedabadcity.gov.in → Tenders Section

Surat (Surat Smart City Development Ltd): suratsmartcity.ctegroup.in ya suratmunicipal.gov.in

Vadodara (VSCDL / VMC): vmc.gov.in

Rajkot (RUDA / RMC): rmc.gov.in

C. Gujarat State Centralized Tender Portals (Bidding Ke Liye)
EOI notice GIL/Smart City ki website par dikhega, lekin documents submit aur bid upload hamesha in platforms par hoga:

(n)Procure Gujarat: eproc.gujarat.gov.in ya nprocure.com

GeM Portal: gem.gov.in (Search filter: Category = IT Services / AI Solutions, Buyer = Gujarat State Govt).

2. EOI / RFP Ke Liye Apne Aap Ko Kaise Taiyar Karein? (Action Plan)
EOI aate hi apply karne ke liye pehle se ye cheezein taiyar rakhein:

Step 1: Digital Signature & Vendor Enrollment
Class 3 DSC (Digital Signature Certificate): Bidding ke liye Dongle/Token hona zaroori hai.

(n)Procure Bidder Registration: eproc.gujarat.gov.in par jaakar Vendor Account bana lein aur DSC map kar lein.

Step 2: Pitch Deck & Company Capability Profile Ready Karein
EOI mein pehla round Technical Evaluation hota hai. Tender document aane par aapko ye submit karna padta hai:

Capabilities Presentation: Aapki firm AI/ML, NLP, Chatbots, ya Software mein kya kar sakti hai.

Case Studies / Past Projects: Kisi private client ya government ke liye kiye gaye similar projects ka proof (Work Orders / Completion Certificates).

Team Profile: AI Engineers, Data Scientists, aur Project Managers ki CVs aur qualifications.

Step 3: MSME & DPIIT Startup Registration (Sabse Zaroori Step)
Agar aapki company Startup ya MSME hai, toh apply karne se pehle ye certificates zaroor lein:

Udyam Registration Certificate (Free online ho jata hai)

DPIIT Startup India Recognition Certificate

Benefit: Govt EOI/RFPs mein Startup India / MSME registered companies ko Turnover Experience, Prior Experience (Relaxation in Criteria), aur EMD Deposit Exemption ki chhoot milti hai. Isse aap badi companies ke sath compete kar sakte hain.

3. Smart Strategy: Tender Alerts Kaise Set Karein?
Roz 10 alag websites manually check karna mushkil hota hai, iske liye 2 best tarike hain:

GeM Custom Keyword Alerts: GeM portal par Seller Account banakar apne profile mein keywords daal dein: AI, Machine Learning, Chatbot, Software Development, System Integrator. Jab bhi Gujarat Govt koi tender nikalegi, aapko email alert aa jayega.

CPPP / State Procurement Email Notifications: eprocure.gov.in aur eproc.gujarat.gov.in par Free Bidder Account banakar "IT/Software Services" category ke email updates active kar lein.
