Question:

Kya ye coincidence hai?

Nahi.

General equation hoti hai:

y=mx+c
	​


Yahan

m = slope
c = intercept (ML me bias b)

Machine Learning me isi equation ko hum likhte hain:

y=wx+b
	​


Yahan:

w = slope (weight)
b = intercept (bias)

Isliye tum dekh rahe the ki Andrew Ng baar-baar bolte hain:

Weight (w) is the slope of the line.
