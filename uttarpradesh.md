1. Key Official Portals (Jahan Sub-Tenders Aur Bids Aate Hain)
A. UP e-Procurement Portal (Main Portal)
Website: etender.up.nic.in

Kya Milta Hai: Uttar Pradesh Govt ke saare departments (IT & Electronics, Urban Development, Transport, PWD, Municipal Corporations) ke sabhi tenders aur RFPs ispar aate hain.

Kya Karna Hai: Yahan par "Bidder Enrollment" karwayein aur Class-3 DSC (Digital Signature Certificate) map karein.

B. GeM Portal (Government e-Marketplace)
Website: gem.gov.in

Kya Milta Hai: UP Govt ke departments se Hardware Procurement (Desktops, Laptops, Servers) aur IT Services/Manpower/AMC ke liye bano tenders aur direct orders.

Filter: State = Uttar Pradesh chun kar IT Services / Custom Software / AI category search karein.

2. UP Govt Ki Key Nodal Agencies (Inpar Nazar Rakhna Sabse Zaroori Hai)
Uttar Pradesh mein IT, E-Governance aur Emerging Tech (AI/ML) projects ke liye ye mukhya agencies hain:

UPDESCO (Uttar Pradesh Development Systems Corporation Ltd.)

Website: updesco.in

Khasiyat: UPDESCO UP Govt ki primary Nodal Agency for IT hai. Saare state-level e-Governance projects, custom software development, IT manpower supply, aur hardware procurement yahi handle karti hai.

Empanelment: UPDESCO time-to-time IT/Software Vendor Empanelment ke liye RFPs nikalta hai. Ek baar empanel hone par bina har baar bada tender bhare aapko software/AI work orders mil sakte hain.

UP Electronics Corporation Limited (UPLC)

Website: uplc.in

Khasiyat: UPLC bhi state govt ke IT infrastructure, E-governance portals, aur technical consultancy ke tenders release karti hai.

Smart City Missions (Municipal Corporations)

UP mein bahut sare Smart Cities hain jahan AI/ML Traffic Systems, AI Cameras, City Apps, Smart Surveillance, aur Citizen Chatbots ke EOIs/RFPs aate hain.

Mukhya Portals:

Lucknow Smart City: lucknowsmartcity.com

Kanpur Smart City: kanpursmartcity.in

Varanasi Smart City: smartcityvaranasi.org

Agra / Prayagraj / Bareilly / Gorakhpur Smart City portals.

3. Uttar Pradesh Ke Tenders Pane Ki Smart Strategy
UP Startup Policy Ka Fayda uthayein:

Agar aapki firm UP Startup / MSME registered hai (ya DPIIT Startup Certificate hai), toh UP Govt Tenders mein aapko:

EMD Deposit Exemption (Security amount jama nahi karna padta).

Prior Turnover & Experience Exemption (Chhoti/Nayi startups badi companies ke saath compete kar sakti hain).

Alerts & Keywords Set Karein:

etender.up.nic.in par apna Free Account banakar category "Information Technology" select karein aur keywords daalein: AI, ML, Software, Web Development, Chatbot, System Integrator.

Sub-contracting / Consortium (Tie-up):

UPDESCO aur Smart Cities ke bade AI/IT Tenders (₹1 Cr+) ke liye aap UP ke bade System Integrators ya L1 bidders ke saath AI/ML Technical Partner ke taur par tie-up kar sakte hain.
